How it works

Up and running in minutes

No technical knowledge needed. If you can use WhatsApp, you can use DelivaGH.

01

Sign up, add your business name, location, and upload your menu. Takes about 5 minutes.

What you get

Everything a food vendor needs

Built specifically for food businesses in Ghana. Not a generic tool adapted for Africa — built here, for here.

WhatsApp ordering bot

Customers browse your menu, add to cart, and confirm orders — all inside WhatsApp. No app download, no website needed.

MoMo payments

Accept payments from MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, and AirtelTigo Money. Customers get an OTP prompt right in the chat.

Order dashboard

See every order in real time. Update status from New → Preparing → Ready and the bot notifies the customer automatically.

Order status updates

Customers always know where their order is. The bot sends automatic updates at every stage so you handle fewer "where is my food" messages.

Menu management

Add, edit, and remove items from your menu anytime from your dashboard. Changes go live on the bot instantly.

Customer recognition

Returning customers are greeted by name and offered their previous order with one tap. Makes reordering effortless.
