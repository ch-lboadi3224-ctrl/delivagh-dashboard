Privacy Policy

We believe your data belongs to you. Here's exactly how DelivaGH collects, uses, and protects your information.

Last updated: May 1, 2026
01 — Introduction

Who We Are

DelivaGH ("we," "us," or "our") is a WhatsApp-based order management platform that connects food businesses in Ghana with their customers. We operate at www.delivagh.app.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our service — whether you are a vendor managing orders through our dashboard, or a customer placing an order through WhatsApp.

By using DelivaGH, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of our service.

02 — Data Collection

Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide our service:

  • Customer data: WhatsApp phone number, order details (items, quantities, prices), delivery or pickup preference, and any customization notes you provide during ordering.
  • Vendor data: Business name, owner name, email address, WhatsApp business phone number, and menu information uploaded to the platform.
  • Conversation data: Messages exchanged through our WhatsApp bot are processed to fulfill orders. Conversation history may be stored temporarily to maintain context within an active session.
  • Usage data: Technical information such as request timestamps, order statuses, and system logs used for debugging and service improvement.
03 — Data Usage

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Process and fulfill food orders placed through WhatsApp
  • Notify vendors of incoming orders in real time via the DelivaGH dashboard
  • Send customers order status updates (e.g., accepted, ready, delivered)
  • Operate and improve the DelivaGH platform and AI ordering bot
  • Authenticate vendor accounts and maintain platform security
  • Comply with applicable laws and regulations in Ghana

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We do not use your data for advertising purposes.

04 — Third-Party Services

WhatsApp & Meta Integration

DelivaGH uses the WhatsApp Business Platform (Meta) to enable customer interactions. When you message our bot, your messages are transmitted through Meta's infrastructure. Please refer to WhatsApp's Privacy Policy for information on how Meta processes messaging data.

We also use the following third-party services to operate our platform:

  • Supabase — database and authentication (data stored in secure cloud infrastructure)
  • Railway — backend hosting and deployment
  • Vercel — vendor dashboard hosting
  • Paystack — payment processing for mobile money transactions
  • Groq / AI providers — powering the intelligent ordering bot

Each of these providers has their own privacy policies and data protection practices.

05 — Data Retention

How Long We Keep Your Data

We retain order data for as long as necessary to provide our service and for reasonable business record-keeping. Vendors may request deletion of their account and associated data at any time by contacting us.

Conversation history used by the ordering bot is stored temporarily and may be cleared when a new session begins or upon customer request.

06 — Your Rights

Your Rights & Choices

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate personal data
  • Request deletion of your personal data
  • Opt out of receiving order status messages (note: this may limit service functionality)
  • Lodge a complaint with a relevant data protection authority

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the details below.

07 — Security

How We Protect Your Data

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These include:

  • Encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) for all data in transit
  • Secure authentication for vendor dashboard access
  • Access controls limiting who can view customer and order data
  • Regular monitoring of our systems for vulnerabilities

No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

08 — Children's Privacy

Children's Privacy

Our service is not directed to individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately.

09 — Policy Updates

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of DelivaGH after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
